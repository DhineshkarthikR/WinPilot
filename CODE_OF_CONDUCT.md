# WinPilot Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The WinPilot project is dedicated to providing an open, welcoming, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or technical environment.

We are committed to maintaining a collaborative, professional, and respectful environment where contributors and users can discuss systems administration, PowerShell engineering, and software security constructively.

---

## Expected Behavior

Members of the WinPilot community are expected to:

- **Demonstrate Respect and Empathy**: Treat fellow contributors, maintainers, and users with dignity and kindness.
- **Focus on Constructive Collaboration**: Provide thoughtful, actionable feedback on issues, code reviews, and discussions.
- **Respect Differing Perspectives**: Welcome diverse viewpoints, experiences, and skill levels.
- **Maintain Professional Integrity**: Uphold the project's security invariants, legal compliance, and zero-tolerance policy regarding unauthorized licensing circumventions.
- **Prioritize Security and Privacy**: Practice responsible disclosure and protect user privacy in all diagnostic and management code.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited within the WinPilot repository, issue tracker, discussions, and associated communication channels:

- Harassment, derogatory commentary, offensive remarks, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or disruptive behavior.
- Publishing or distributing private personal information or full product keys without authorization.
- Promoting, requesting, or submitting code for unauthorized software activation, KMS bypasses, or digital rights circumvention.
- Spamming, automated promotional content, or unsolicited commercial advertising.

---

## Enforcement and Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the authority to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that do not adhere to this Code of Conduct.

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers through GitHub Private Reports or by opening a confidential discussion with the repository owners. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled discreetly and impartially.

---

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct reflects common open-source community standards and project-specific governance designed to foster transparent, secure, and professional collaboration.
